What holds it in place?

The cap flange stays inside the wall. The separate holder supports the switch; its rear face carries the pressing load. The bridge prevents the switch lifting out. Two M2 × 18 mm machine screws pass through the bridge and holder into M2 nuts under the base. Nothing is force-snapped into the printed base.

Print a trial first

Print the S04 coupon, one side holder, one side cap and one retaining bridge. Remove supports and burrs. The switch must enter from above without bending its pins or spreading the holder. Check the purchased screw length, nut size and free return before using the full base.
