Assembly

  1. Print the S01 base (with two integral cradles), two side caps and two retainers. Keep the existing C01 lid and six front buttons.
  2. Insert each side cap from inside. Its Ø14.8 mm flange prevents pull-out through the Ø12.6 mm wall opening.
  3. Wire the switch before fitting. Seat its body against the rear support, with the actuator facing the cap. Lead escape channels remain open towards the PCB.
  4. Fit the retainer with two M2 × 6 mm plastic self-tapping screws per button. Do not overtighten. The body—not its solder leads—takes the press load.
  5. Verify continuity, free return and the hard stop with the real switch before closing the lid.
