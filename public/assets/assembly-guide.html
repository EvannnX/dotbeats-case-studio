The S03 base is identical to S02 and can be reused. Use the new S03 lid, keyed caps and offset carriers as a matching set. The six visible caps are centered; PCB and USB positions are unchanged. Do not mix C01 caps/carriers with the keyed S03 lid. Disconnect the battery during assembly.

01 · Prepare and test-print

Print one S03 base (or reuse the S02 base), one S03 lid, six S03 keyed caps, six S03 offset carriers, two side caps, two retainers and three PCB centering bushes. Remove supports and burrs. First test one front button and the side fit coupon with real switches and motors.

Check: All sliders move freely. Confirm your motor and switch dimensions before printing the full set.

02 · Install the enclosure inserts

With electronics and battery removed, heat-set four M2.5 inserts (outer Ø4.6 × 4 mm long) into the underside ends of the lid posts. Keep the inserts square and let the plastic cool. Nominal printed pocket: Ø4 × 5 mm deep.

Check: Inserts are seated at the post ends, perpendicular, and do not protrude. Test the thread with an M2.5 screw by hand.

03 · Fit the three PCB nuts

Turn the base over. Put one M2.5 hex nut (5 mm across flats, 2 mm thick) into each of the three underside hex pockets. Push each nut against its pocket ceiling. Temporarily hold them with removable tape while turning the base upright.

Check: Three nuts are in the PCB positions A, B and C. These are not the four corner enclosure screws.

04 · Install the two side buttons

Insert each side cap through the wall from inside. Solder and insulate the switch wires before seating the switch sideways against the cradle rear wall. Fit the retainer with two M2 × 6 mm plastic self-tapping screws. Identify the two contact pairs using a meter; use one terminal from each pair.

Check: Both caps return freely and give one clean switch closure. Pilot-hole fit and the nominal 0.50 mm hard stop require a physical trial.

05 · Lower the PCB onto its posts

With the power disconnected, lower the complete PCB / directly soldered ESP assembly into the base. Align the three existing Ø5.2 mm PCB holes with posts A, B and C. The PCB underside rests at z = 4.2 mm above the outer bottom surface.

Check: All three holes line up and the PCB sits flat. No wire or underside component is trapped under a post. The USB connector remains aligned with its opening.

06 · Center and screw down the PCB

Drop one printed centering bush (Ø4.8 outer / Ø2.8 inner × 1.5 mm high) into each PCB hole. Add one insulating washer (Ø7 outer / Ø2.7 inner × 0.5 mm), then an M2.5 × 6 mm pan-head machine screw. Start all three screws before gently tightening into the underside nuts.

Check: The PCB cannot slide or rock, but is not bent. Nominal nut engagement is 1.90 mm; screw tips stay 0.30 mm above the outer bottom. Do not substitute longer screws.

07 · Assemble the six front keys

Insert each keyed front cap from outside the lid, with its outer rib aligned to the matching lid slot. Place its motor in the cavity, with the wires aligned to the relief slot. Fit the offset carrier/plunger from inside, aligning its keyed groove with the inner cap key and matching both wire slots. Do not force or rotate it into another orientation. Secure only the intended cap-to-carrier joint using a plastic-compatible adhesive, keeping adhesive away from the guide and motor wires.

Check: Each cap slides and reaches both stops without rotating. The offset stem must remain above its original PCB switch. Do not bond any moving part to the lid. Allow adhesive to cure before testing.

08 · Fit the battery and route wires

Seat the insulated battery in the existing cradle. Secure it with thin, removable battery-retention tape compatible with the cell wrap; the cradle alone is not a positive lock. Route motor and side-switch wires around the PCB edge with slack for button travel. Follow the actual PCB wiring and polarity; this model does not define the electrical pinout.

Check: The cell cannot move, the wrap is undamaged, no bare conductor is exposed, and no wire enters a screw pocket, sliding guide or lid seam. Check clearance after adding retention material.

09 · Lower and fasten the lid

Keep the lid parallel and lower it slowly, checking all six plungers align with their PCB switches. Stop if anything binds or a switch stays pressed. Once the lid rests evenly, insert four M2.5 × 8 mm machine screws through the base from underneath into the lid inserts. Tighten gradually in a diagonal sequence.

Check: The seam closes without forcing it. Use the 8 mm screws only at the enclosure corners; the PCB uses 6 mm screws.

10 · Final functional check

With power still disconnected, check free return and continuity of all eight buttons. Confirm the USB opening, screws and battery retention. Then connect power using the approved circuit and test each button and motor individually. If a key sticks, disconnect power and correct the fit rather than tightening the case harder.

Check: No permanent switch preload, loose PCB, trapped wire or moving battery. Model checks do not replace an actual fit, repeated-press and electrical test.

Print and verification notes

FDM starting point: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.16–0.20 mm layers, four perimeters. Base floor down. Inspect bridging over underside nut pockets, clear the pilot holes and test a single bush before printing three. Never tighten screws to force a misaligned PCB into place.

Verified digitally: three hole centres and approximately Ø5.2 mm hole boundaries in the supplied PCB; unchanged 4.2 mm PCB underside height; watertight one-piece base; nut-pocket clearance; no modeled component collision with the new PCB fasteners. Nominal nut engagement is 1.90 mm and screw-tip clearance above the outer bottom is 0.30 mm.

Not physically verified: FDM fit, torque, repeated assembly, button reliability, actual solder/wire envelopes and electrical pinout. The original motor Ø10 × 4 mm and front-button travel remain reference assumptions. Prototype one button first. Screw, bush and nut geometry must match purchased hardware.

Reference switch drawing: Omron B3F. These files do not replace the actual PCB circuit diagram.
