Assembly order

01 · Build the buttons

Insert the caps through the lid from outside. Place each motor inside its cap, then fit the carrier and plunger from inside. Bond the cap-to-carrier joint; keep the guide surfaces clear.

02 · Fit the electronics

Place the PCB / ESP assembly and battery in the base. Route wires clear of moving parts. The plunger tips align with the six tactile switches.

03 · Close the enclosure

Heat-set four inserts into the lid posts. Lower the lid onto the base and fasten it with four screws from underneath. Verify every button moves and returns freely.
